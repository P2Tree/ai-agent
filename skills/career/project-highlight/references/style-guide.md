# Style Guide

## Universal Rules (All Scenarios)

1. **No fabrication** — never invent metrics, achievements, or responsibilities not evidenced by git data or user confirmation. Mark uncertain items as "待确认".
2. **Quantify everything possible** — "389 commits over 4 years" beats "长期贡献"; "支持 5 代硬件设备" beats "多代设备支持".
3. **Use domain-accurate terminology**
4. **Default language: Chinese** unless user requests English. Technical terms may stay in English when no precise Chinese equivalent exists.

## Resume (简历)

- **Voice**: Third person implied, drop subject ("实现了..." not "我实现了...")
- **Tense**: Past tense for completed work
- **Length**: 3-5 bullets, each 1-2 lines
- **Prioritize**: Impact > Action > Detail
- **Each bullet must contain a number or concrete scope**
- **No**: adjectives without data ("大幅提升", "显著优化")
- **Yes**: "性能提升 X%", "覆盖 N 个模块", "支持 M 代设备"
- **Order**: Most impactful contribution first

## Interview (面试/口头描述)

- **Voice**: First person, conversational
- **Tense**: Mix — past for completed, present for ongoing
- **Length**: 2-3 minutes spoken (~400-600 Chinese characters)
- **Prioritize**: Story arc (challenge → approach → result)
- **Show reasoning**: Why this approach, what alternatives were considered
- **Prepare for follow-up**: Each point should have a deeper story underneath
- **No**: reading from script, overly formal language
- **Yes**: natural flow, genuine technical enthusiasm, honest about challenges

## Performance Review (述职)

- **Voice**: First person but formal
- **Tense**: Past with forward-looking future
- **Length**: Structured document, 1-2 pages
- **Prioritize**: Impact metrics > activity lists
- **Tables required** for contributions and plans
- **No**: modesty framing ("只是做了..."), vague plans
- **Yes**: "完成了 X, 影响 Y 范围, 对标 Z 标准", concrete future targets

## General Introduction (通用介绍)

- **Voice**: Neutral, informative
- **Tense**: Present for ongoing, past for completed milestones
- **Length**: 1-2 paragraphs (~200-300 Chinese characters)
- **Prioritize**: Breadth of contribution > depth of any single item
- **Accessible**: Non-experts should understand what the project does
- **No**: deep jargon, long technical explanations
- **Yes**: brief context for technical terms, balanced scope/depth

## Technical Writing Principles

- One idea per sentence
- No filler words (很, 非常, 显著, 大幅) without data
- Parallel structure in bullet lists
- Active voice preferred
- Abbreviations defined on first use
